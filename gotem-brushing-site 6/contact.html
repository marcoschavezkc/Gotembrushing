Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can you start?

Most projects can be scheduled within 1–2 weeks of your estimate, depending on scope and season. Rush requests are handled case by case.

Do you provide the paint, or should I buy it?

We supply premium, low-VOC paint as part of our quote. If you have a specific brand or color already picked out, we're happy to use it.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — we're fully licensed and carry liability insurance. Certificates are available on request for commercial and HOA jobs.

Do you offer a warranty on the work?

Every job includes a workmanship guarantee. If something isn't right after we finish, we'll come back and make it right.
