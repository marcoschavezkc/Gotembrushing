Our Painting Services

Full-service interior, exterior, and commercial painting — plus the prep work and specialty finishes that make it last.

Interior Painting

Walls, ceilings, trim, doors, and cabinetry — precise cut-ins and smooth, even coverage in every room.

  • Living areas, bedrooms & kitchens
  • Trim, doors & crown molding
  • Color consultation available

Exterior Painting

Siding, fascia, shutters, and doors coated with weather-resistant paint built for the elements.

  • Power washing & surface prep
  • Caulking & minor wood repair
  • Premium exterior-grade coatings

Commercial Painting

Offices, retail spaces, warehouses, and multi-family properties — scheduled to minimize disruption.

  • After-hours & weekend scheduling
  • Multi-unit & property management jobs
  • Certificate of insurance on request

Cabinet Refinishing

Give kitchens and bathrooms a full facelift without the cost of a full remodel.

  • Sanding, priming & spray finishing
  • Durable, washable finishes
  • Hardware removal & reinstall

Deck & Fence Staining

Protective stains and sealants that bring out the wood grain and guard against weather damage.

  • Cleaning & light sanding prep
  • Solid or semi-transparent stains
  • Fences, decks & pergolas

Drywall Repair & Prep

Patch holes, smooth texture, and prep problem walls so the paint goes on flawless.

  • Hole & crack patching
  • Texture matching
  • Primer & sealer application
Our Process

What to Expect When You Book With Us

1. Walkthrough

We assess the space, discuss color and finish options, and answer your questions.

2. Written Quote

A itemized quote with materials, labor, and timeline — no surprise add-ons later.

3. Protect & Prep

Furniture covered, surfaces cleaned, patched, and sanded before a drop of paint goes on.

4. Paint & Inspect

Careful application, daily cleanup, and a final walkthrough to confirm you're happy.
